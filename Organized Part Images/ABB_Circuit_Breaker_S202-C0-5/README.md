# 2CDS252001R0984 - ABB S202-C0.5 Miniature Circuit Breaker

**Part number:** `2CDS252001R0984`  
**Manufacturer:** ABB  
**Category:** Miniature circuit breaker (S200 series, 2-pole, C-curve, 0.5 A)  
**Verified:** Yes (links verified May 2026)

## Description
ABB S200 series miniature circuit breaker, 2-pole, C-tripping characteristic, rated current 0.5 A. DIN-rail mounted, 6 kA breaking capacity (IEC 60898-1), 10 kA short-circuit capacity (IEC 60947-2). Used for protecting low-current circuits in industrial and commercial panels.

## Key specifications
Poles: 2; Tripping curve: C; In: 0.5 A; Icn: 6 kA (IEC 60898-1); Icu: 10 kA (IEC 60947-2); Mounting: DIN rail (35 mm); Width: 2 modules
