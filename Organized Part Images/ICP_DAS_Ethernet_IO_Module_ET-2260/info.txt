ICP DAS PART

Part number       : ET-2260
Product name      : ICP DAS ET-2260 Ethernet I/O Module
Manufacturer      : ICP DAS
Category          : Ethernet (Modbus TCP) digital I/O module
Verified          : Yes (links verified May 2026)

DESCRIPTION
-----------
ICP DAS ET-2260 industrial Ethernet I/O module with 6 digital inputs and 6 relay (Form A) digital outputs. Supports Modbus TCP and DCON protocols. Built-in web configuration, 10/100 Mbps Ethernet, isolated DI/DO, DIN-rail mount.

KEY SPECIFICATIONS
------------------
DI: 6 channels (isolated); DO: 6 Form A relays; Communication: Ethernet 10/100 Modbus TCP + DCON; Power: +10 to +30 V DC; Mount: DIN rail
