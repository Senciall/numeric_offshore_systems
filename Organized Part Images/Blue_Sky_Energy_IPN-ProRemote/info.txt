SUNFORGE LLC (FORMERLY BLUE SKY ENERGY) PART
========================================
Part number       : IPN-ProRemote-S
Product name      : Blue Sky Energy / SunForge IPN ProRemote
Manufacturer      : SunForge LLC (formerly Blue Sky Energy)
Category          : Remote display / system controller
Verified          : Yes (links verified May 2026)

DESCRIPTION
-----------
Wall-mounted remote display and system controller for Blue Sky Energy / SunForge Solar Boost iL/iX series MPPT charge controllers. Provides full system monitoring, battery state-of-charge, charge stage indication, amp-hour and watt-hour logging, and configuration of charge parameters via the IPN bus.

KEY SPECIFICATIONS
------------------
IPN bus interface; LCD display + buttons; Battery monitoring (SoC, Ah, Wh); Compatible with Solar Boost 1524iX, 2512iX, 3024iL, 3024DiL; Flush or surface mount
