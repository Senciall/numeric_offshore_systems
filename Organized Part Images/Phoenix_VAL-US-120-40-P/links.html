2910335 / 2910342 - Phoenix Contact VAL-US-120/40-P + GDT-US-NG/40-P

Phoenix Contact - Type 1 surge protection plug + gas discharge tube replacement plug

Phoenix Contact VAL-US-120/40-P (item 2910335) is a Type 1 replacement surge protection plug for 120 V AC single-phase supplies, varistor-based, In = 20 kA, Imax = 40 kA. GDT-US-NG/40-P (item 2910342) is the matching gas discharge tube (N-G path) replacement plug for the VAL-US base. Both plug into the VALVETRAB US base element.
Specs: VAL-US-120/40-P: UC 175 V AC; In 20 kA (8/20 us); Imax 40 kA; Up <= 0.9 kV; W 17.5 mm; IP20. GDT-US-NG/40-P: N-G path GDT; Imax 40 kA; matches VAL-US base.
Notes: Two-part folder: VAL-US-120/40-P (2910335) and GDT-US-NG/40-P (2910342). Both plug into VALVETRAB US base.
