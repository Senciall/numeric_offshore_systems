Verified          : Yes (links verified May 2026)

DESCRIPTION
-----------
Phoenix Contact PLUGTRAB PT 5-HF-12 DC-ST (item 2838775) is a pluggable surge protection device optimized for high-frequency (HF) data and information-technology signal circuits. 5-line protection at continuous operating voltage UC = 14 V DC and nominal signal current IN = 450 mA, with low capacitance suitable for higher data rates. Plugs into a PT-BE/S base element on a 35 mm DIN rail; replace without disconnecting field wiring.

KEY SPECIFICATIONS
------------------
Lines: 5; UC: 14 V DC; UN: 12 V DC; IN: 450 mA; In (8/20 us): 5 kA; Up: < 35 V (line/line), < 600 V (line/PE); Status indicator: mechanical disconnect indicator; Mounts on PT-BE base; W 6.2 mm per plug
