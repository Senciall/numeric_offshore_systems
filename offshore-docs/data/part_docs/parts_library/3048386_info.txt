DESCRIPTION
-----------
Fuse holder for 10 × 38 mm fuse-links (per IEC 60269-2). 690 V AC, 32 A, single-pole disconnect feed-through. Screw connection, black.

KEY SPECIFICATIONS
------------------
Fuse: 10×38 mm ceramic; Nominal voltage: 690 V AC; Nominal current: 32 A; Rated cross section: 16 mm² (1–16 mm²); Mounting: NS 35/7,5 or 35/15; Color: black
