IPN-ProRemote-S - Blue Sky Energy / SunForge IPN ProRemote

SunForge LLC (formerly Blue Sky Energy) - Remote display / system controller

Wall-mounted remote display and system controller for Blue Sky Energy / SunForge Solar Boost iL/iX series MPPT charge controllers. Provides full system monitoring, battery state-of-charge, charge stage indication, amp-hour and watt-hour logging, and configuration of charge parameters via the IPN bus.
Specs: IPN bus interface; LCD display + buttons; Battery monitoring (SoC, Ah, Wh); Compatible with Solar Boost 1524iX, 2512iX, 3024iL, 3024DiL; Flush or surface mount
Notes: Blue Sky Energy is now SunForge LLC.
