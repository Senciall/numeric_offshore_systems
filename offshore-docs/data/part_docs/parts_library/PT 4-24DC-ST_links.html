2839240 - Phoenix Contact PLUGTRAB PT 4-24DC-ST Surge Protection Plug

Phoenix Contact - Pluggable surge protection for measurement and control (MCR) signals - 4 lines, 24 V DC

Phoenix Contact PLUGTRAB PT 4-24DC-ST (item 2839240) is a pluggable Type 3 / Class III surge protection device for measurement and control (MCR) signal circuits. 4-line protection (e.g., two pairs of differential signals) with a continuous operating voltage UC = 27 V DC and nominal signal current IN = 2 A. The protection plug snaps into a PT-BE/S base element on a 35 mm DIN rail. Plug visually indicates its status; replace the plug without disconnecting field wiring at the base.
Specs: Lines: 4; UC: 27 V DC; UN: 24 V DC; IN: 2 A; In (8/20 us): 5 kA; Up: < 50 V (line/line), < 50 V (line/PE); Status indicator: mechanical disconnect indicator; Mounts on PT-BE base; W 6.2 mm per plug
Notes: Pluggable module - requires a Phoenix Contact PT-BE / PT-BE/S base element (sold separately) to mount and wire. Usually used together with PT 5-HF-12DC-ST (item 2838775) on the same DIN rail in MCR/control panels.
