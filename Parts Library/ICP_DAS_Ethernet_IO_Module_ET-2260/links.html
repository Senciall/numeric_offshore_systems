ET-2260 - ICP DAS ET-2260 Ethernet I/O Module

ICP DAS - Ethernet (Modbus TCP) digital I/O module

ICP DAS ET-2260 industrial Ethernet I/O module with 6 digital inputs and 6 relay (Form A) digital outputs. Supports Modbus TCP and DCON protocols. Built-in web configuration, 10/100 Mbps Ethernet, isolated DI/DO, DIN-rail mount.
Specs: DI: 6 channels (isolated); DO: 6 Form A relays; Communication: Ethernet 10/100 Modbus TCP + DCON; Power: +10 to +30 V DC; Mount: DIN rail
