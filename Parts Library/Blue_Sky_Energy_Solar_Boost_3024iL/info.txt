SUNFORGE LLC (FORMERLY BLUE SKY ENERGY) PART
========================================
Part number       : SB3024iL
Product name      : Blue Sky Energy / SunForge Solar Boost 3024iL MPPT Charge Controller
Manufacturer      : SunForge LLC (formerly Blue Sky Energy)
Category          : MPPT solar charge controller (12/24 V, 40 A)
Verified          : Yes (links verified May 2026)

DESCRIPTION
-----------
Maximum Power Point Tracking (MPPT) solar charge controller, 12 V or 24 V battery operation (auto-detect), 40 A maximum charge current. IPN bus for networked monitoring and accessory connection. Used in off-grid solar systems with up to ~600 W (12 V) or ~1200 W (24 V) of PV input.

KEY SPECIFICATIONS
------------------
Battery: 12 V / 24 V (auto); Max charge current: 40 A; Max PV input: ~600 W @12V / 1200 W @24V; MPPT efficiency: typ. 99 percent; IPN bus: yes; Conformal-coated PCB
